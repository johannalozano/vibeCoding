## Show & Swap read

_What a partner understood from your build with no verbal setup, their reaction is your first piece of product evidence._

- **What they understood immediately:** At the start it was hard, he thought it was a restaurant dashboard because of the colors
- **What confused them:** The color schema was very plain, so it was hard to read
- **The assumption they thought you were testing:** After looking it, it's a to-do list in a CRM tool
- **The gap between what you intended and what they read:** The gap is the input and refinement with care and detail which I didn't do for this Lab 1, so the first version felt disconnected
