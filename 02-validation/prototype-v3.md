# Prototype (v3): The Build That Tests the Hypothesis

> Module 2 · Validation. The prototype is a hypothesis test, not a demo.

https://cue-bright.lovable.app

## What it tests

This prototype tests whether **reframing the CRM as a prioritized daily action queue — instead of a data-entry system — makes the Customer Success team more willing to use it as part of their daily workflow**.

Specifically, it puts this assumption in front of users:

**If the CRM immediately tells CS what needs attention today, why it is prioritized, and lets them take common actions with minimal friction, they will see it as useful for doing their job rather than as administrative work for management.**

The prototype tests whether users can quickly:

* Understand **what to work on next**
* Trust the prioritization based on **committed date and account value**
* Navigate a mixed workload of **deals, onboarding, support, and follow-ups**
* Complete routine actions without opening a long CRM form
* See the CRM as a viable replacement for their shadow spreadsheet

---

## Context injected (no placeholders)

* **Real user quotes on screen:**

  * “It’s faster to keep my deals in a spreadsheet than to fight the CRM’s eight required fields.”
  * “Logging activity feels like data entry for management, not something that helps me sell.”
  * “If it could just tell me who to call next, I’d open it every morning.”

* **Domain metrics on screen:**

  * **18%** weekly active adoption across licensed seats
  * **8 required fields** to log a single ticket or account update
  * **11 min** average task time to update a deal or ticket vs. a **2-min target**
  * **63%** of reps maintain a shadow spreadsheet alongside the CRM

The prototype itself also makes the prioritization model tangible through:

* **Left / Overdue / Done** workload counters
* Ranking by **committed date first, then account value**
* Visible explanations such as **“3 days overdue · $188k account”**
* Time, work-type, and stage/status filters
* Inline actions including **Log Call, Add Note, Update Status, Snooze, and Complete**
