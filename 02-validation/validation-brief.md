# Validation Brief

**Scenario:** _(name your scenario)_

## 1 · Hypothesis
I'm testing whether if we switch the way we interact with the CRM our internal team will actually adopt it and use it in their daily tasks

## 2 · Risk type & kill switch
- **Risk type:** Value
- **Build to test it:** Landing page
- **Kill switch:** If when displaying this to the Customer Success colleagues, more than 50% of them don't understand at a glance what the Daily Queue screen is for→ premise was wrong, pivot the CRM workflow.

## 3 · Success criteria
More than 50% of the internal team integrates the CRM Dialy Queue functionatily into their daily task workflow

## 4 · Problem Framework
1. **Goal**, Improve CRM adoption in the internal team
2. **Problem**, The CRM does not support the way the team currently works. It does not have the data nor the interaction they'd like to see
3. **Context**, The specific internal user is the Customer Success team. They need to figure out quickly which task they need to tackle next, if it's a support task or an customer onboarding task for example. Today the tasks emerge in different sources, email from a customer, Slack message from another colleague or the CRM itself when a ticket is opened in the support inbox. So also creating a screen where they can consolidate everything and follow on the tasks from opening to closed.
4. **Constraints**, We can't really change the CRM tool per se, the goal is to improve it.
5. **Success**, More than 50% of the internal team integrates the CRM feature into their daily task workflow
6. **Explore**, On the way as we create the correct CRM workflow, we will learn where does the team source the data for their tasks and also how the follow up with the different data sources separately today.
